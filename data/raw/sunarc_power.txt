SunArc Power is a renewable-energy company headquartered in Chennai. Its CEO is Vikram Rao. In March 2025 SunArc acquired GreenGrid Energy. In June 2025 SunArc announced a partnership with NovaTech Labs to build smart meters using NovaTech's NT-7 chip.

SunArc Power was founded in 2011 and has about 650 employees. The company operates 14 solar plants with a combined capacity of 1.8 gigawatts. SunArc Power reported revenue of $410 million in fiscal year 2025. SunArc Power plans a pilot of 500,000 smart meters in Tamil Nadu in 2026. Isha Verma joined the SunArc Power board in April 2025 after the GreenGrid Energy deal closed.
