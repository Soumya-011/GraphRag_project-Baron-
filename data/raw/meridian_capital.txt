Meridian Capital is a venture capital firm based in Mumbai. Its managing partner is Rohan Kapoor. Meridian Capital has invested in NovaTech Labs and in GreenGrid Energy. Rohan Kapoor joined the NovaTech board in 2022 after the Series B round. The firm manages about $600 million.

Meridian Capital was founded in 2010 and focuses on deep-tech and clean-energy startups. Its first fund, raised in 2011, was $50 million. Rohan Kapoor has led Meridian Capital since 2015. Meridian Capital made a $15 million Series A investment in GreenGrid Energy in 2016. Meridian Capital currently holds about 22 portfolio companies. After the SunArc Power acquisition of GreenGrid Energy, Meridian Capital reported a 3.1x return on that investment.
