# NovaTech Labs
NovaTech Labs is an edge-AI chip company headquartered in Pune, India. It was founded in 2018 by Aarav Mehta and Priya Nair. Aarav Mehta is the CEO and Priya Nair is the Chief Technology Officer. In 2022, NovaTech raised a $40 million Series B round led by Meridian Capital. The company has 180 employees and its flagship product is the NT-7 inference chip.

## Products
The NT-7 is built on a 12 nm process and draws under 3 watts at full load. NovaTech Labs shipped the NT-7 to its first customers in January 2023. A successor, the NT-9, was announced in September 2025 and is expected to deliver twice the performance of the NT-7.

## Team and customers
Kavya Iyer joined NovaTech Labs as head of manufacturing in 2021, after six years at Orbit Systems. NovaTech Labs reported revenue of $28 million in 2024. In June 2025, NovaTech Labs partnered with SunArc Power to supply the NT-7 chip for smart meters.
