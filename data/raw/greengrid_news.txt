GreenGrid Energy, a Bengaluru-based smart-grid startup, was acquired by SunArc Power in March 2025 for $120 million. GreenGrid's CEO Isha Verma will join the SunArc Power board after the deal. Before the acquisition, Meridian Capital was GreenGrid's largest investor. Priya Nair, now CTO of NovaTech Labs, worked at GreenGrid as a lead engineer from 2013 to 2017.

The GreenGrid Energy platform monitors more than 2 million smart meters across Karnataka and Tamil Nadu. GreenGrid Energy had 95 employees at the time of the deal. SunArc Power paid for the acquisition entirely in cash. SunArc Power said it will keep the GreenGrid brand for one year. GreenGrid Energy reported revenue of $18 million in 2024.
